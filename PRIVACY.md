# OrcaPad privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

OrcaPad is a 3D-printing slicer for iPhone and iPad. It does not collect,
store, sell or share any personal data, and it has no accounts, analytics,
advertising or tracking.

## What stays on your device

- Models, projects, G-code and presets you create or import are stored in the
  app's own storage on your device (visible in the Files app under
  "On My iPhone/iPad > OrcaPad") and are never sent to the developer.
- App settings (for example your last selected printer and presets) are
  stored locally on your device.
- Printer connection details you enter (address, API key) are stored locally
  on your device.

## Network access

OrcaPad only connects to the 3D printers you configure yourself, usually on
your local network. It sends them the files you choose to print and shows the
printer's own web interface. It does not contact any server run by the
developer.

## TestFlight and the App Store

When you test OrcaPad through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports,
usage information and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy
policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer only sees what
Apple shares through App Store Connect, such as crash reports and feedback you
submit.

## Children

OrcaPad does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
